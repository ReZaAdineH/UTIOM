# Licensing

The UTIOM Framework is published as fully open material. Two licences apply, because a framework and the software that implements it need different terms.

## Summary

| What | Licence | File |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Framework content — all `.md` documentation, the framework book (PDF), diagrams, assessment criteria, maturity and capability models, metric definitions | **CC BY-SA 4.0** | [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) |
| Software — the assessment tools (HTML/CSS/JavaScript), any scripts, the Dockerfile, packaging | **Apache License 2.0** | [`LICENSE-CODE`](LICENSE-CODE) |

If a file's category is ever unclear, treat it as framework content under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Why two licences

Creative Commons licences are designed for creative and documentary works, and Creative Commons itself recommends against applying them to software — they do not address patent grants, source distribution, or warranty disclaimers in the way software licences do. Apache-2.0 covers those properly and is widely accepted inside enterprises.

Using the right licence for each category is not pedantry. It is what allows a bank's legal team to approve using the tools internally without a review cycle.

## What CC BY-SA 4.0 means for the framework

You are free to:

- **Share** — copy and redistribute the material in any medium or format
- **Adapt** — remix, transform, and build upon the material
- **For any purpose, including commercially**

Under two conditions:

- **Attribution** — you must give appropriate credit, link to the licence, and indicate if changes were made
- **ShareAlike** — if you remix, transform, or build upon the material, you must distribute your contribution under the same licence

This means a consultancy may use UTIOM in client work, a vendor may build on it, and a university may teach it — provided derivative *framework material* stays open under the same terms. UTIOM cannot be absorbed into a closed, proprietary model.

## How to attribute

Minimum acceptable attribution:

> Based on the UTIOM Framework — Unified Threat-Informed Operations Model by Reza Adineh, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. https://utiom.de

For academic or formal citation:

> Adineh, R. (2026). *UTIOM Framework — Unified Threat-Informed Operations Model* (Version 1.1). Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. https://utiom.de

If you have modified the material, say so — for example: "Adapted from UTIOM v1.1; the maturity thresholds have been modified."

## What the licences do not cover

Licences cover copyright. They do not grant rights to the **name**. See [`TRADEMARK.md`](TRADEMARK.md) for the naming policy that applies to forks and derivative works.

## Contributions

Contributions are accepted on an inbound-equals-outbound basis: by submitting a contribution you agree it is licensed under the same terms as the part of the project it touches — CC BY-SA 4.0 for content, Apache-2.0 for code. See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
