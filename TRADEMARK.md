# Naming and attribution policy

UTIOM is open material. This document is about the **name**, not the content.

The open licences in [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md) grant broad rights to copy, adapt and redistribute the framework and the tools. Those are copyright licences. They do not grant permission to use the project's name in a way that suggests something is the official, canonical UTIOM when it is not.

The reason for this policy is simple: if several incompatible things all call themselves "UTIOM", the term stops meaning anything, and everyone who has adopted the framework loses. Keeping the name stable is what keeps the open material useful.

## The canonical version

**UTIOM**, **UTIOM Framework** and **Unified Threat-Informed Operations Model** identify the framework published by Reza Adineh at [utiom.de](https://utiom.de) and in this repository. The version published there is the canonical one.

Note on usage: *UTIOM Framework* is the preferred descriptive form in prose. *Unified Threat-Informed Operations Model* is the canonical expansion of the acronym and should always appear exactly as written, with the hyphen, and without "Framework" appended to it.

## What you may do without asking

- State that your work is **based on**, **adapted from**, **compatible with**, **aligned to** or **implements** UTIOM
- Use the name in comparative, descriptive, educational, academic or journalistic contexts
- Cite UTIOM, quote it, teach it, and reproduce it under the terms of CC BY-SA 4.0
- Say that your product, service or SOC **uses** UTIOM
- Fork this repository and modify it

## What requires a different name

- Distributing a **modified** version of the framework under the name "UTIOM" without clearly marking it as modified and unofficial
- Naming a product, service, company, domain or certification in a way that implies it is the official UTIOM (for example "UTIOM Pro", "UTIOM Certified", "UTIOM Institute")
- Presenting a derivative work as **the** UTIOM framework

If you publish a modified version, please make it obvious. For example: "Acme SecOps Model, based on UTIOM v1.1 (modified)".

## Certification and training

There is no official UTIOM certification body, certification programme, or accredited training. Nobody is authorised to issue UTIOM certifications. If that changes it will be announced at [utiom.de](https://utiom.de). You are welcome to teach and train on UTIOM — just don't describe it as official or accredited.

## Getting in touch

If you want to use the name in a way this policy does not clearly permit, or you are unsure, ask first via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ReZaAdineH/UTIOM/issues) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rezaadineh/). The default answer to good-faith requests is yes.
